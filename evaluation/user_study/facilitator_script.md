# Facilitator script

Read the parts in quotation marks aloud so every participant hears the same thing.

**Introduction**
"Thank you for helping. This is a tool I built for revising from your own study material. I'm testing the tool, so there are no wrong answers. Please think out loud if you can. I won't help unless you get really stuck, because I want to see where the design is unclear."

Make sure **Use the example material** is switched on in the sidebar, and that the page shows step 1 only.

---

**Task 1: Process the material** (start timing when you finish reading)
"Imagine these are your notes, a lecture recording and a slide from a computer architecture module. Please get RevisionFlow to process them."

Success: the participant clicks **Process material** and step 2 appears.

---

**Task 2: Check and correct what the AI understood**
"Before generating anything, look at what the AI understood from your material. The slide text has some lines that are not real words. Please remove them, then create the revision material."

Success: the nonsense lines at the top of **Slide text (OCR)** are deleted, and **Generate revision material** is clicked.

---

**Task 3: Revise and export**
"Use the quiz to answer any two questions and check your answers. Then save the flashcards so you could use them in another app."

Success: two questions answered and checked, and **Download flashcards** clicked.

---

**Close**
"Thank you. Please fill in the short questionnaire now."
