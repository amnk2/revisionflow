# Participant information sheet

**Study:** Usability of RevisionFlow, a revision tool that turns study material into quiz questions and flashcards
**Researcher:** BSc Computer Science student, University of London (CM3070 Final Project)

## What is this study about?
I have built a program that uses AI models to turn lecture audio, slide screenshots and notes into revision material. I would like to find out whether it is easy to use and whether its features help people check the AI's work.

## What will I be asked to do?
You will use RevisionFlow for about 15 minutes to complete three short tasks with example study material that I provide. Afterwards you will fill in a questionnaire of 17 questions. In total it takes about 20 minutes.

## Do I have to take part?
No. Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any point without giving a reason. You can also ask for your answers to be removed until the report is submitted, by quoting your participant ID.

## What information is collected?
- Whether you completed each task, and how long it took.
- Your questionnaire answers.

Your name is not recorded with your answers; you are given an ID such as P3. Nothing is recorded on video or audio. You do not upload any of your own files. The answers are stored on my computer and used only for my final project report, where results are reported anonymously.

## Are there any risks?
There are no known risks. The study tests the software, and there are no right or wrong answers.

Please keep this sheet.
