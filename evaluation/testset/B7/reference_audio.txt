Let's go through the three laws. The first law says that a body stays at rest, or keeps moving at a constant speed in a straight line, unless a force acts on it. This tendency to resist changes in motion is called inertia. The second law says that the net force on a body equals its mass multiplied by its acceleration, which we write as F equals m a. Equivalently, force is the rate of change of momentum. The third law says that when two bodies exert forces on each other, the forces are equal in size and opposite in direction. So when you push against a wall, the wall pushes back on you just as hard.
