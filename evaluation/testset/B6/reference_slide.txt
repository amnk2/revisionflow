Capacity of working memory
Capacity is limited: only a few items can be held at once
Miller (1956): the magical number seven, plus or minus two
Later estimates are lower: about four chunks (Cowan, 2001)
Chunking groups items into larger meaningful units
Capacity differs between individuals
It is linked to reading comprehension and reasoning
Measured with complex span tasks, such as reading span
Capacity develops through childhood
Capacity tends to decline in older age
