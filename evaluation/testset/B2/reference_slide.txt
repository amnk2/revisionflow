The Calvin cycle (light-independent reactions)
Takes place in the stroma of the chloroplast
The enzyme RuBisCO captures CO2 from the atmosphere
CO2 combines with ribulose 1,5-bisphosphate (RuBP)
This produces two molecules of glycerate 3-phosphate
Uses the ATP and NADPH made in the light-dependent reactions
Glycerate 3-phosphate is reduced to glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate
Five out of six molecules are used to regenerate RuBP
The remaining triose phosphates form sucrose and starch
Sugars are also used for cellulose and cellular respiration
